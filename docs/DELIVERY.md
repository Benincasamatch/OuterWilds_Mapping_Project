## 使用 / Use

直接打开本目录的 `index.html` 可在现代浏览器预览。WE 项目入口是同目录 `project.json`，请保持文件夹完整。

Open `index.html` in a hardware-accelerated WebGL2 browser. For Wallpaper Engine, keep the folder intact and use `project.json` as the project entry.

左键拖动环绕，右下角 + / − 按钮缩放、⌂ 重置视角；浏览器中可用滚轮，中键拖动平移。默认空闲 15 FPS、交互 30 FPS，同时遵守 WE 全局帧率上限。属性面板可调时间倍率、画面比例、轨道线、星空亮度和暂停恢复模式。

Left drag orbits; lower-right + / − buttons zoom and the home button resets the view. Browser wheel zooms; middle drag pans. Default frame limits: 15 FPS idle / 30 FPS active, also bounded by the host's global cap. Use the property panel for time scale, visual scale, orbit lines, brightness and resume behavior.

**剧透警告 / Spoilers:** includes base-game and DLC locations and time-dependent changes. 1360-second cycle including the tail. Not a recreation of every in-game event.
